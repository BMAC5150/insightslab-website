Key Interview Questions

What are the most critical data sources and reports you rely on for your day-to-day decisions?
Purpose: Identify high-priority data assets and decision-making dependencies
Expected Insight: Critical data sources inventory and decision support gaps
How is data currently collected, stored, accessed, and shared within your team and across the organization?
Purpose: Map existing data lifecycle and workflow processes
Expected Insight: Current data flow patterns and process inefficiencies
What are your main challenges or pain points with the current data ecosystem (e.g., quality, availability, timeliness, relevance)?
Purpose: Uncover specific data management obstacles and frustrations
Expected Insight: Priority pain points for immediate resolution
How do you currently collaborate or share data with other departments or external stakeholders?
Purpose: Understand cross-functional and external data exchange requirements
Expected Insight: Collaboration gaps and integration opportunities
If you could improve or automate any single aspect of your data management or reporting in the next year, what would it be—and why?
Purpose: Identify highest-impact automation and improvement opportunities
Expected Insight: Quick win priorities and strategic focus areas
