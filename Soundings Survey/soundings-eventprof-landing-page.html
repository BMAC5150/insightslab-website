Key Strategic Insights

  • • Industry AI Maturity Score: 2.6/5: Business events industry trails behind banking (4.0), manufacturing (4.2), and even healthcare (3.1).
  • • Process vs. Tools Adoption: Organizations that redesign workflows see 10× greater returns than those just deploying tools.
  • • Adoption Personas: Five distinct adoption personas identified, with Tech-Forward Planners (25%) showing 32% productivity improvements.
  • • Training Crisis: 76% of respondents rank training as their top requested resource, yet only 23% of organizations have formal training programs.
