Assessing Workflow Transformation Potential

5-Point Evaluation Framework

Strategic value: Direct impact on business outcomes
Resource intensity: Time, money, and staff resources required
Transformation potential: Opportunity for improvement
Technology readiness: Data availability and structure
Team readiness: Alignment with adoption personas

Strategic Workflow Prioritization Process

Inventory core business event workflows
Catalog and categorize all workflows that drive organization outcomes (pre-workshop task)
Rate each workflow on the 5 assessment criteria
Apply the evaluation framework to score each workflow for transformation suitability
Identify top-candidate workflows for transformation
Select 1-2 high-scoring workflows with greatest potential business impact
Map current state of high-potential workflows
Document each step, decision point, and handoff in existing workflow
Analyze steps for AI augmentation or replacement
Identify which workflow components can benefit from AI assistance or automation
Design future-state workflow with AI integration
Reimagine workflow to incorporate AI while maintaining quality and human oversight
Implement with appropriate team involvement and measurement
Roll out new workflow with training, collect before and after metrics to demonstrate value

Executive Action Planning for Workflow Transformation

Days 1-15: Prioritize and Form Team
Identify 1-2 workflows for transformation and assemble a cross-functional team that includes both tech-forward and relationship-focused members
Days 16-30: Map and Identify
Document current workflow states in detail and identify specific steps where AI can augment human capabilities rather than replace them
Days 31-60: Design and Implement
Create transformed workflows and gradually introduce them with appropriate training and mentorship for team members
Days 61-90: Measure and Refine
Collect performance metrics, gather team feedback, and make necessary adjustments to optimize the transformed workflows
