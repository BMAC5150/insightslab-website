1

Contact Import & Enrichment

Upload tradeshow contact list to the secure Insights Lab Platform for processing and enrichment.

  • System automatically checks for duplicates
  • Contact data is enriched with additional firmographic information
  • Data is validated and standardized for consistency
2

AI-Powered Lead Qualification

The system analyzes each lead using AI to evaluate potential value and conversion likelihood.

  • AI evaluates each contact based on:
  • Company size and profile match
  • Industry alignment with DMO's target segments
  • Job title and decision-making authority
  • Historical conversion patterns

AI-Generated Lead Insights and Reports

  • ✓Conversion probability score
  • ✓Recommended follow-up timing
  • ✓Personalized talking points
  • ✓Content recommendations based on prospect needs
3

Segmentation & Follow-up Planning

Leads are automatically categorized based on potential value and assigned appropriate follow-up strategies.

  • Leads are automatically segmented into categories:
  • Hot leads (high potential, immediate follow-up)
  • Warm leads (moderate potential, scheduled follow-up)
  • Nurture leads (long-term potential, automated campaign)
  • AI suggests optimal follow-up timing for each segment
  • Relevant content is matched to each segment automatically
4

Personalized Outreach Execution

AI-generated customized follow-up engagement plans for each lead, reviewed by the sales team, can then be generated.

  • EXAMPLE: For high-value leads, AI generates customized follow-up/engagement action plans:
  • Specific tradeshow conversation references
  • Company-specific value propositions
  • Relevant case studies and success stories
  • Sales team reviews recommended plans, edits, and activates accordingly
  • Leads for nurturing receive automated communication sequences
  • All interactions are logged in CRM with follow-up tasks scheduled
5

Performance Tracking & Optimization

The Insights Lab system can monitor performance and suggest improvements over time.

  • Analyzes patterns in successful conversions
  • Tradeshow ROI is calculated based on lead conversion
  • AI model improves with each interaction and campaign

Key Benefits

  • •Focus on high-potential leads: Sales team spends more time with prospects most likely to convert
  • •Reduced administrative work: Automation eliminates manual research and data entry tasks
  • •Higher quality follow-up: Personalized communications generate better response rates
  • •Strategic prioritization: Ensures promising leads receive prompt attention while others are nurtured appropriately
  • •Clear ROI tracking: Complete visibility into tradeshow-generated pipeline and conversions
  • •Continuous improvement: Lead scoring models get smarter with each tradeshow and interaction
  • •No missed opportunities: Consistent follow-up ensures no leads fall through the cracks
