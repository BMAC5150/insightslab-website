Key Insights from Survey Data

Adoption Trends

  • The Pragmatic Adopter is the most common persona (28%), indicating a value-focused industry approach to AI adoption
  • Tech-Forward Planners report productivity improvements up to 32%, highlighting AI's potential value in event planning
  • Content creation (83%) and marketing (76%) are the most common AI applications, showing initial focus on creative tasks
  • 73% of survey respondents (across all personas) plan to increase AI use in the next 12 months

Implementation Challenges

  • Training and education is the most requested resource (76%), indicating widespread knowledge gaps
  • Experience correlates with adoption approach - veterans often either embrace tech (Tech-Forward) or resist it (Relationship-Focused)
  • Event size impacts technology adoption - larger events (500+ attendees) correlate with higher AI usage
  • The industry shows classic late-adopter characteristics compared to sectors like manufacturing and banking

Survey conducted of business event professionals in February-March 2025
