Strategic Targeting Approaches

  • Financial Services Focus (Tier 1)

    Target Financial Services leads in October (peak pickup month) for booking with event execution in May (peak satisfaction month). Emphasize premium accommodations and 5-star service with target ADR of $425-435.

  • Healthcare/Pharmaceutical Initiative (Tier 1)

    Develop specialized campaign targeting Healthcare prospects in February (peak pickup) with June execution dates (highest satisfaction ratings). Focus on California and East Coast organizations with ADR of $385-395.

  • Technology Sector Opportunity (Tier 1)

    Create October-focused initiatives for Technology sector leads, when both pickup and satisfaction rates peak simultaneously. Target California and Washington-based tech companies with emphasis on premium incentive travel packages.

  • Manufacturing & Event Management (Tier 1)

    Highlight September (peak satisfaction) and October (peak pickup) when approaching Manufacturing sector and Event Management prospects. Develop specialized FAM trips during these optimal months to showcase the DESTINATION experience.

  • Automotive Sector Strategy (Tier 1)

    Create specialized October-November showcase opportunities for Automotive clients to maximize both pickup (October) and satisfaction (November). Target Missouri-based organizations and major automotive incentive programs with ADR of $385.
