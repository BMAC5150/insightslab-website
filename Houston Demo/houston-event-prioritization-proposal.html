The Houston Opportunity

The planned 700,000-square-foot expansion of the George R. Brown Convention Center represents a transformative moment for Houston's business events industry. With the expansion scheduled to open in May 2028, now is the critical time to:

Key Opportunity

  • Develop a high-value event pipeline aligned with the new capabilities
  • Create strategic partnerships with event organizers who will benefit from the expanded facilities

The Challenge

Houston competes in an increasingly competitive convention marketplace where cities across North America are expanding facilities and increasing marketing budgets. Without strategic prioritization of sales efforts, your team will engage with hundreds of prospects but may miss the highest-value opportunities.

Expansion Highlights

The Phase One development, GRB Houston South, will feature 150,000 square feet of exhibition space, a 50,000-square-foot multipurpose hall, a 25,000-square-foot atrium flex hall, and the largest ballroom in Texas (60,000-80,000 square feet) with panoramic downtown views.
