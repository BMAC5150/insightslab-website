IMEX Frankfurt is one of the world's premier exhibitions for global meetings, events, and incentive travel, attracting over 9,000 buyers from agency, corporate, and association sectors. This represents both an opportunity and a challenge:

Opportunity

Access to thousands of global meeting planners

Challenge

With a vast marketplace of planners and hundreds of global competitors, Los Cabos must precisely target who needs to be engaged with before, during, and after IMEX in order to generate results
