AI Advantage Workshop: FIEXPO Latin America

AI-Enhanced Business Event Lead Generation for DMOs and Venues

Workshop Overview

This interactive half-day workshop equips Latin American DMOs and convention venue professionals with practical AI skills to enhance lead generation and business event sales performance. Participants will learn how to leverage AI tools to identify high-value prospects and implement data-driven strategies to close more business.

Duration: 3 hours (Half-day)

Target Audience

  • DMO Business Events Sales Directors and Managers
  • Convention Venue Sales Teams
  • Marketing Directors and Managers
  • Business Development Professionals

Pre-Workshop Activities

  • Personal AI Readiness Assessment (distributed via QR code)
  • Participants to bring meeting planner guides and venue collateral
  • Technical preparation (ChatGPT access, laptop required)
Industry Reality Check
Business events industry AI maturity score: 2.6/5, trailing far behind banking (4.0), manufacturing (4.2), and healthcare (3.1). This creates both a challenge and an opportunity for Latin American DMOs and venues to gain competitive advantage.
