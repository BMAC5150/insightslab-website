How the 0-100% Scoring System Works

The 9-factor scoring system that predicts your win probability

9 destination parameters

The Matching Challenge

You found 30 potential events. Great! Now what? Which ones should you actually pursue? Which justify premium subvention? Which are worth a site visit? Which should you pass on?

Without clear scoring, you're guessing. With BidScout's matching, you're deciding with data.

The 9-Factor Scoring System

1. Industry Alignment (25%)

Do you have the companies, associations, institutions, and expertise this event needs? A biotech conference needs biotech companies. An AI summit needs AI startups and researchers. BidScout measures the depth of your industry ecosystem match.

2. Venue Capacity Fit (15%)

Can you physically host this event? Meeting space, hotel rooms, ballroom size—matched against their requirements. BidScout flags when you're perfect, when you're close enough with nearby support, or when it's unrealistic.

3. Transportation Access (12%)

Can attendees get to you easily? BidScout analyzes flight connectivity, international access, ground transportation, and cost factors against where their members typically travel from.

4. Academic & Research Alignment (10%)

For conferences with educational components: Do your universities and research institutions match their focus areas? This often breaks ties between otherwise similar destinations.

5. Rotation Eligibility (10%)

Are you even in the running? Many events rotate between continents, regions, or avoid recent host cities. BidScout checks rotation patterns and flags red lights before you waste time.

6. Business Intent Alignment (10%)

Does this event match your destination's strategic goals? If you're trying to grow medical tourism, a medical conference scores higher than an unrelated event—even if both technically fit.

7. Accommodation Quality (8%)

Do you have the right types of hotels? A luxury association needs upscale properties. A grassroots nonprofit needs affordable options. Quality match matters as much as quantity.

8. Safety & Security (5%)

For international events: visa requirements, safety reputation, health infrastructure. For all events: emergency services, medical facilities, political stability factors.

9. Event Confidence (5%)

How confident is BidScout in the event data? Verified database events score higher than newly discovered opportunities. You know the difference between a sure thing and a maybe.

What Your Score Means

85-100%: Excellent match. Priority bid. You check nearly every box. Allocate premium resources.

70-84%: Strong match. Solid opportunity. You meet most requirements with some strategic advantages. Worth pursuing.

60-69%: Good match. Viable opportunity. You can compete but face some challenges. Consider bid based on competition.

40-59%: Moderate match. Possible but challenging. Pursue only if competition is weak or strategic value is exceptional.

Below 40%: Poor match. Pass unless you have insider relationships or unique circumstances.

Result: Won 5 of 12. Previous win rate: 1 in 30.
