THSCA Strategic Opportunity

Texas High School Coaches Association Convention - Visit Frisco Analysis

Executive Summary

The THSCA Coaching School & Convention represents a major strategic opportunity for Visit Frisco to elevate its position as Sports City USA. While Frisco cannot independently host the full 18,000-attendee event, it can play an indispensable leadership role in a visionary DFW regional bid, leveraging its premier sports identity, hotel infrastructure, and high-value venue cluster.

The Bottom Line

Frisco is not a standalone host for the full THSCA Coaching School—but it can play an indispensable leadership role in a visionary DFW regional bid. As Texas' epicenter for professional-grade sports experiences, Frisco elevates the overall brand, enhances event value, and supports a long-term relationship with THSCA leadership. With proactive engagement and strategic coalition-building, Visit Frisco can anchor the "sports experience" layer of Texas' most important high school coaching convention.
